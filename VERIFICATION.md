## What the proof must establish

For every complex zero of the Riemann zeta function, after excluding the pole at 1 and the trivial zeros −2, −4, −6, …, its real part equals ½. Mathlib explicitly excludes 1 because its zeta function assigns a value there. The first target is classical RH. The release also states results for Dirichlet L-functions. Alpöge describes the Extended Riemann Hypothesis as a prospective extension. [Mathlib v4.33.1 definition](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/0df444a360eaa60ab8c11dca51a86af692955474/Mathlib/NumberTheory/LSeries/RiemannZeta.lean) · [Original entry point](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Main.lean) · [Announcement, p. 1](./remark.pdf)

## Following the mathematics

Start at `riemannHypothesis` in `Main`. Its proof is `rhOfAll`, which applies `Formalism.VW.rh_of_every` to `allChi`. The reduction specializes the Dirichlet nonvanishing theorem to the character modulo 1 and rewrites its L-function as zeta. This gives two concrete questions for reading the proof. How does right-half-plane nonvanishing imply classical RH? Where does the proof establish nonvanishing for every character? [Main theorems](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Supplements/Kernels/MainTheorems.lean) · [Modulus-one specialization](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/GammaFactorNonvanishing/CritLine.lean)

**Checked final implication.** We isolated the original declarations `rh_of_right`, `UpperTheory.EveryCharacter`, and `rh_of_every` and compiled them with Mathlib. A separate challenge stated the Dirichlet nonvanishing premise explicitly. Con-leche accepted 53,448 solution declarations and matched the resulting implication to that challenge. The same solution also passed Comparator with the Lean default kernel, the paranoid kernel and nanoda. Lean reported dependencies on `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`. The premise in this check is what the full proof must establish. [Reproduce the reduction](verification/check-reduction.sh) · [Con-leche verdict](verification/evidence/reduction-compare.log) · [Comparator verdicts](verification/evidence/reduction-comparator.log)

Three source landmarks connect the announcement to the mathematics in the release. These identifications come from reading the original declarations.

- **From prime powers to zeta zeros.** `Formalism.Sz` sums Λ(n)(x/n − 1) over integers from 1 through ⌊x⌋. Subtracting x log x and adding (1 + γ)x gives `Formalism.Gz`. The theorem `zetaLandau` proves a conditional implication. For 0 < θ < 1, an eventual upper bound Cx^θ on either Gz or −Gz implies that ζ has no zeros with real part greater than θ. [Weighted sum](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/StatementOC.lean#L11) · [Exact hypotheses](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/StatementU.lean#L14) · [Proof of the implication](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/ZetaNonvanishing/ZetaLandau.lean#L258)
- **How long is the chain?** `LiftChain` relates the forms at levels j and j + 1 for each j < m. `SizeM` puts m between x^(−ε/100)√(xδ) and x^(ε/100)√(xδ), with δ = x^(−ε/8). This gives a precise version of the square-root scale in the announcement. The proof uses the upper bound in `m_pow_four_le_n` to show m⁴ ≤ n under its stated size assumptions. [Lift recurrence](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/Divisibility/ZetaProductPairs.lean#L240) · [Definition of δ](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/DataParams₂.lean) · [Bounds on m](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/ElevenLe.lean#L27) · [Use of the bound](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/FourthPowerRankLeNF.lean)
- **How divisibility accumulates.** At a ramified place, `atD_of` applies `ValuationComposition` to the one-lift estimates in `OneLiftAtDiscriminant`. The resulting lower bound adds the contributions across j = 1 through m with the stated error terms and correction. This is one explicit instance of passing from a per-lift bound to a bound on the chain. [Composition rule](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/Divisibility/OrbitPlaces.lean#L30) · [Resulting bound](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/Divisibility/OrbitPlaces.lean#L440) · [Proof](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/Divisibility/OrbitPlaces.lean#L1078)
