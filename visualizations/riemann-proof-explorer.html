How the sum connects to zeros
The release calls the weighted sum Sz and its error from the smooth main term Gz. A bound on one side of that error, valid for every sufficiently large X, gives a zero-free half-plane through zetaLandau.
  1. Rule out zeros to the right of ½
    allChi supplies nonvanishing for every Dirichlet character when Re(s) > ½, with the pole excluded. This is the main mathematical input. The author’s note describes iterated theta lifts and divisibility in this part of the argument.
  2. Specialize to the zeta function
    rh_of_every takes the character modulo 1. Its L-function equals ζ, so ζ cannot vanish to the right of the critical line away from its pole.
  3. Reflect a hypothetical zero on the left
    For a nontrivial zero inside the strip, the functional equation would give another zero at 1 − s. That point lies in the right half, where the previous step rules it out. The Lean lemma rh_of_right also checks the exceptional factors.
